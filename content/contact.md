## Getting in touch

This site doesn't run a contact form, comments section, or analytics, so reach me through one of the following:

- **GitHub** — issues and pull requests are welcome on any of my public repositories
- **Email** — [cajunhacker@gmail.com](mailto:cajunhacker@gmail.com) — for site corrections or technical questions

## What I'm happy to talk about

- Mistakes or errors in anything I've written here
- Pointers to better resources on topics I cover
- Homelab setups and lessons learned
- Cybersecurity learning paths and mentorship for vets transitioning into IT/security

## What I won't engage with

- Unsolicited sales, SEO, or marketing pitches
- Requests for help with anything that looks unauthorized
- "Quick favors" that turn into unpaid consulting

## Disclosure & responsible reporting

If you've found a security issue with this site or any project I publish, please report it privately first. I'll respond within a reasonable window and credit you appropriately if you'd like.
