Welcome to C@junH^ck3r.

This site documents my cybersecurity learning, self-hosting projects, homelab builds, bug bounty notes, and technical education resources. The audience is other practitioners — anyone who wants the rough edges left in and the marketing taken out.

The first project on display is the site itself: a Hugo-built static site, served by Nginx on a small VM at home, exposed publicly via a Cloudflare Tunnel. No database. No admin panel. No tracking. The full write-up is in [How I Self-Hosted This Website with Nginx and Cloudflare Tunnel]({{< ref "self-hosted-nginx-cloudflare-tunnel" >}}).
