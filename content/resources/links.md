Bookmarks that have earned their place. Updated when something stops being useful.

## Code hosting & open source

- [GitHub](https://github.com/) — where most of my public code, issues, and PRs live; the default hub for modern open source
- [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/) — long-running open-source host; still useful for older or niche projects you won't find on GitHub

## Operating systems & distros

- [Ubuntu](https://ubuntu.com/) — what I run on servers and most lab VMs; long-term support releases keep maintenance predictable
- [Kali Linux](https://www.kali.org/) — the pentest distro; useful as a disposable attacker VM, not as a daily driver
- [Parrot OS](https://parrotsec.org/) — Debian-based security distro; lighter than Kali, good alternative if Kali doesn't fit your workflow

## Official references

- [OWASP](https://owasp.org/) — Top 10, Cheat Sheets, ASVS, SAMM
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) — adversary tactics and techniques, the common vocabulary
- [NIST SP 800 series](https://csrc.nist.gov/) — the actual standards behind a lot of US compliance
- [CIS Benchmarks](https://www.cisecurity.org/cis-benchmarks) — hardening baselines for almost everything
- [Linux man pages](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/) — when in doubt, RTFM

## Vulnerability and threat intel

- [CVE](https://cve.mitre.org/)
- [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/)
- [CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)
- [Project Zero blog](https://googleprojectzero.blogspot.com/) — the writing is part of the lesson

## Reading and writeups

- [The DFIR Report](https://thedfirreport.com/) — detailed real-world incident writeups
- [HackerOne Hacktivity](https://hackerone.com/hacktivity) — disclosed reports, sharpens your sense for what real findings look like
- [Pentester Land newsletter](https://pentester.land/) — curated pentest writeups
- [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/) — the working journalist of infosec

## Tools and references

- [GTFOBins](https://gtfobins.github.io/) — Unix binaries that can be abused for privilege escalation
- [LOLBAS](https://lolbas-project.github.io/) — the Windows equivalent
- [HackTricks](https://book.hacktricks.xyz/) — a sprawling but useful reference for offensive techniques
- [PayloadsAllTheThings](https://github.com/swisskyrepo/PayloadsAllTheThings) — payload cheat sheets
- [SANS Cheat Sheets](https://www.sans.org/posters/) — surprisingly good free posters

## Self-hosting

- [Awesome Self-Hosted](https://awesome-selfhosted.net/) — the canonical curated list
- [r/selfhosted](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/) — community knowledge, varies in quality
- [Cloudflare Tunnel docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/) — the actual docs, which are good
- [Tailscale documentation](https://tailscale.com/kb/) — likewise good

## Cybersecurity literature

News sites, magazines, and podcasts I check regularly. Skim the headlines; read the ones that matter.

- [Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com/) — Jack Rhysider's narrative podcast on hackers, breaches, and cybercrime. Long-form storytelling, not headlines
- [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/) — fast-turnaround breach and vuln reporting; one of the most-read infosec news sites
- [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/) — daily aggregation of vulns, breaches, and threat actor activity
- [Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/) — enterprise security reporting and analysis; Informa-owned, long-running
- [Threatpost](https://threatpost.com/) — vulnerability and threat reporting; archive is still useful even though publishing has slowed
- [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/) — industry news, ICS/OT coverage, M&A
- [Security Magazine](https://www.securitymagazine.com/) — broader enterprise security trade publication (physical + cyber)
- [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/) — UK-based, conference-adjacent, good European coverage
- [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/) — daily news plus product/research roundups
- [IT Security Guru](https://www.itsecurityguru.org/) — UK industry news and opinion
- [Cyware](https://cyware.com/cyber-security-news-articles) — threat-intel-flavored news aggregation
- [WIRED — Security](https://www.wired.com/category/security/) — mainstream tech journalism with real depth on big stories (Greenberg, Newman, et al.)
