## About this site

C@junH^ck3r is a working notebook. The goal is to write down what I learn while doing cybersecurity work, building out a homelab, and self-hosting services — in plain English, with the rough edges left in. If something here saves another practitioner a few hours, the site is doing its job.

The site is intentionally simple: static HTML generated by Hugo, served by Nginx, fronted by a Cloudflare Tunnel. No database, no comments, no tracking. Source is plain Markdown.

## About me

I'm **Scott Landry**, a retired U.S. Army Lieutenant Colonel. Most of my career was in the Signal and Cyber branches, with roles spanning:

- Signal operations and communications planning
- Cybersecurity and cyber operations
- COMSEC and information assurance
- Knowledge management
- Training development and evaluation

I'm a **Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)** and have spent enough time on both the defensive and offensive sides of the house to know that the boring fundamentals — identity, patching, logging, segmentation, backups — beat clever tools every time.

These days I focus on:

- Hands-on cybersecurity practice and bug bounty research
- Building a Proxmox-based homelab for training and experimentation
- Self-hosting services for myself and family on a small, well-documented stack
- Writing things down so others can skip the mistakes I made

## What you'll find here

- **Blog** — write-ups, build logs, and notes
- **Education** — structured learning paths I wish someone had handed me earlier
- **Projects** — real infrastructure I run, documented so it's reproducible
- **Resources** — tools, books, links, and training platforms worth your time
